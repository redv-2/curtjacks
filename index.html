Hi! My name is Curtis.

I see every song and groove as more than just a track; I see an experience. Music lets you lose yourself, your worries, even make you feel stronger than you ever knew. Music can free your mind to be its truest self, or just reminds you of a great day.
The importance of a DJ especially in a world of cold, robotic norms is to make sure you're capturing that feeling and making your night special, with the perfect soundtrack accompanying it. This is where I can light up your night and make your night burned into memory forever.
Below is a quote that really resonates with the free spirited nature of music that resonates with me and why I enjoy music.

"No one should be embarrassed by sh-t they like that’s dumb. Just like what you like. Who cares?"
-Chino Moreno, Deftones (Lead Singer)
